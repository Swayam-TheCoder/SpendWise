Rate Limiting for Authentication

Stop someone from repeatedly doing:
POST /login
POST /login
POST /login
POST /login


Solution:

10 requests
    ↓
within 15 minutes
    ↓
same client/IP
    ↓
blocked temporarily

we don't rate-limit /me here because It's already protected by the access token.

Why logout doesn't need this limiter?
Logout is generally harmless:
POST /logout
Calling it repeatedly doesn't help an attacker much.

After the configured limit, you will receive:
{
  "success": false,
  "message": "Too many authentication attempts. Try again later."
}
